===============================================================================
  QA AUTHORING STUDIO  —  ARCHITECTURE & FLOW
===============================================================================
  How a feature selection becomes a validated suite of test cases.
  Truth flows from the Compiler · reasoning from Claude · every result is
  validated before it is trusted.

  LEGEND (ownership):
    [1] COMPILER  — owns product truth        [3] CLAUDE   — reasoning engine
    [2] QA STUDIO — assemble & validate        [4] OUTPUT   — trusted deliverable
    (SKILLS = how Claude reasons; holds no product facts)
